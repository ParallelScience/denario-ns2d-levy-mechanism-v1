**Title**: Disentangling Lévy Flights from Vortex Trapping in 2D Turbulence via Lagrangian-Vorticity State Partitioning

**Description**: This study identifies the source of anomalous diffusion by partitioning the 8,000 tracers into "vortex-trapped" and "strain-dominated" populations based on their proximity to local vorticity maxima across the 15 available Eulerian snapshots. We define a tracer as trapped if its local vorticity $\omega(x,y,t)$ exceeds a threshold $\sigma_{\omega}$ consistently over consecutive snapshots, allowing us to compute conditional displacement PDFs $P(\Delta x, \tau)$ for each sub-population. By comparing the characteristic function decay index $\alpha(\tau)$ of the trapped population against the strain-dominated population, we isolate whether heavy tails are a property of the background flow or a consequence of intermittent vortex ejection. We further analyze the velocity increment PDF $\delta v(\tau)$ specifically during transitions between high-vorticity and low-vorticity regions to determine if large-scale jumps coincide with exit events from coherent structures. This methodology rigorously tests the CTRW mechanism against the direct Lévy-flight hypothesis by determining if the heavy-tailed displacement statistics vanish when tracers are restricted to the strain-dominated, non-vortical regions of the flow.
