The dataset description contains a constraint that limits the reliability of the "Trapping Time Analysis" (Research Plan step 7) and the "Tracer State Partitioning" (Research Plan step 3). 

The Eulerian vorticity snapshots are provided at a temporal resolution of $\Delta t = 40.0$, while the Lagrangian trajectories are sampled at $\Delta t = 0.4$. The research plan relies on linear temporal interpolation of the vorticity field to assign tracer states. Because the turnover time of coherent vortices in 2D turbulence is often significantly shorter than the 40-unit interval between snapshots, the interpolation likely fails to capture the birth, death, and rapid movement of vortex structures. This introduces significant misclassification noise in the "Vortex" vs. "Strain" partitioning. Consequently, the conclusion that trapping times follow a power law ($\mu \approx 0.568$) and the quantitative assessment of vortex-exit events are likely artifacts of the coarse Eulerian temporal sampling rather than purely physical properties of the flow.
